How to read this

This page answers two simple questions about AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and others: which ones are people actually using, and how smart are they. It doesn’t show prices.

Popularity (# and Token share)
How much each model actually gets used. Rank #1 is the most used.
What’s a token?
A token is how AI usage gets counted. It’s a small piece of text, about three-quarters of a word. So 1,000 tokens is roughly 750 words, about a page and a half. Both sides count: your question and the AI’s answer. So a 50-word question that gets a 700-word answer back is about 1,000 tokens for that model. More tokens means people are leaning on that model more. Think of it like counting miles driven instead of cars sold: it shows how much a model is really being used, not just how many people tried it once.
The usage numbers come from OpenRouter, a service that lets apps and developers use hundreds of different AI models in one place. “Token share” is the slice of all the tokens there that went to each model. If a model has 20%, one out of every five words run through OpenRouter that day went through it.
ECI (Epoch Capabilities Index)
A “how smart is it” score from Epoch AI, a nonprofit that studies AI. They give models lots of hard tests in math, coding, science, and reasoning, then boil the results down to one number. Higher means more capable. It works a bit like a school grade: a few points apart is close, a big gap is a real difference.
Arena Score
A “which answer do people like better” score from LMArena. Real people ask a question, get answers from two unnamed models side by side, and vote for the better one. Millions of votes add up to this score. Higher means people preferred its answers more often.

ECI measures test results and Arena Score measures people’s preferences, so a model can rank high on one and lower on the other. We show both as they are and never blend them into one number.

Popularity vs. intelligence

Each dot is one model. The further up a dot is, the smarter the model (higher ECI score). The further right, the more people use it. It has nothing to do with price. Dots in the top right are both smart and popular. Hover or tap a dot to see its name.

Questions and answers

What is the most popular AI model right now?
hy4 Flash from Tencent. It was the most-used model on OpenRouter on the latest full day of data, handling 42.1% of all tokens there. Popular means most used, not smartest. Checked November 28, 2026.
What is the smartest AI model by ECI right now?
Claude Fable 5.2 from Anthropic, with an ECI of 173.2, the highest of the models on this page as of November 28, 2026. Arena Score can rank a different model first, because it measures which answers people prefer, not test results.
What is a token?
A token is how AI usage gets counted. It is a small piece of text, about three-quarters of a word, so 1,000 tokens is roughly 750 words. Both your question and the AI's answer count.
What's the difference between ECI and Arena Score?
ECI, from the nonprofit Epoch AI, is a test score: models take hard tests in math, coding, science and reasoning, and the results become one number. Arena Score, from LMArena, is a people score: real people compare two unnamed answers and vote for the better one. A model can do well on one and less well on the other, so this page shows both and never blends them.
Does this show prices?
No. This page only shows how much each model is used and how capable it is. It has nothing to do with price.
How often is it updated?
Once a day, automatically. The date at the top of the page shows the last update.
Where does the data come from?
Usage comes from OpenRouter, a service that lets apps use hundreds of AI models in one place. Intelligence scores come from Epoch AI (ECI) and LMArena (Arena Score). All three share their data under the CC BY 4.0 license.
Is it free?
Yes. The site is free, has no ads, and needs no sign-up.
